# Code of Conduct

MAP-IT-ALL is a small tool made to be useful, and the people who use and improve it should be able to do that without friction.

## Our standard

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive. "This breaks on Windows paths, here is a fixture" beats "this is broken".
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Respect that people have different experience levels, schedules, and first languages.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space that is part of this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If someone is behaving in a way that breaks this standard, report it privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or by using GitHub's "Report content" on the comment. Reports are handled promptly and treated confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this standard. The full Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines are a good reference for how we think about proportionate responses.
